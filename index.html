STEP 01 · UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM

Two Sum

EASYARRAYSHASH TABLE

You’re given a list of integers and a target. Find the indices of two different numbers that add up to that target.

You can assume there is exactly one answer. Return the indices in any order.

EXAMPLE 01
nums = [3, 2, 4]target = 6↓output = [1, 2]

The values at indices 1 and 2 are 2 and 4. Together they make 6.

CONSTRAINTS TO NOTICEAt least 2 numbers · One guaranteed answer · No reusing an index
